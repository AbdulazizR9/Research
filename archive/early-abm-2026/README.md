# Early Agent-Based Modeling Explorations

**Period:** February 2026  
**Status:** Archived exploratory work

This archive preserves my early attempts to formulate a research question in agent-based modeling.

I initially explored how factors such as confidence, social pressure, and emergency conditions might influence an agent's choice between alternatives A and B.

The notes reflect preliminary thinking, proposed hypotheses, and early plans rather than validated scientific conclusions.

## Contents

- `plan.md` — Initial research interests, questions, and ideas.
- `original_notes/phase-1-pre-relaunch.md` — Early formulation of a binary-decision modeling problem.
- `historical-relaunch-notes.md` — Documentation written during the July 2026 reassessment of the project.
