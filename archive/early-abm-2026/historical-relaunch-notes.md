# Legacy Research Materials

This directory preserves materials from the initial research cycle conducted
before the July 2026 project relaunch.

The original project explored how confidence, social pressure, and environmental
conditions might influence binary choices between alternatives A and B.
A later forensic review found that the intended research framework had not been
fully implemented or experimentally validated.

These materials are retained to document the development of the research idea,
including incomplete assumptions, early planning, and the point at which
implementation was paused in favor of clarifying the scientific question.

## Contents

### `original_notes/`

Early research notes and provisional questions written before the relaunch.

These files represent exploratory thinking rather than a validated research
specification. Statements, hypotheses, and proposed mechanisms contained here
should not be interpreted as current conclusions.
